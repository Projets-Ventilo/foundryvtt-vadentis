# Vadentis — Asset licensing notes

This file records redistribution rights for third-party assets bundled with the Foundry VTT system.

Before a public release, every bundled third-party image, font, sound, map, icon, or other asset must either:

- be original Vadentis material whose redistribution is authorized by the rights holder;
- have an explicit redistribution license compatible with this release; or
- be removed from the public package.

The release cleanup for 2.0.4 removes the previously bundled MP3 music and sound-effect files whose redistribution provenance was not established. The small engineering/alchemy WAV effects remain because they are used by the system; their provenance should still be documented here before public distribution if they are not original assets.

Do not add third-party assets to a public release without recording their source, author, license, and any required attribution in this file.
